# Content policy

This page will state what is never committed: no photograph of the real dog without the
maintainer's approval of that photograph and every metadata field stripped, nothing from
the reference art folder, neither commercial font, and nothing from the rejected or
intermediate generated sets. It will label the generated illustrations as generated and
record that the licence of the studio's own assets is an open question.
