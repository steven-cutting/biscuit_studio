# Quality philosophy

This page will explain why each gate exists and what it asserts: the lockfile check, the
read only hook gate, the static analysis of the site, the coverage floor, the build, the
asset manifest that proves every byte under `assets/` is listed and unchanged, and the two
contracts over the handbook and the agent files. It will say why a check is read only and
why a suppression must carry its reason.
