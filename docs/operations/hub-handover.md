# Hub handover

This page will record what the hub and the games still have to change and what they owe
this repository: the decision and the two design pages that must permit renders from the
approved model before anything leaves the studio, each asset waiting to be promoted with
its path, sha256 and commit, and the receiving repository's own rules about where the
copy lands. Items are prose grouped by shape, never checkboxes.
